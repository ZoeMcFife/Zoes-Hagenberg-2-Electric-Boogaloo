## Pipeline summary (3–5 sentences)

The face portrait and pose reference get put into the image and image_kps inputs of the ApplyInstantID node. Here the InstantID model gets loaded and the Face Analysis node will determine the facial features. Ihe InstantID node also recieves the positive and negative prompts that all get fed to the K-Sampler which goes into Decoder and the image gets outputted. ControlNet is responsible for the structure of the pose, while InstantId preserves the facial identity. The two get combined independendly.
